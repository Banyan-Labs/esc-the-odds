Phase 1 SOW \- Escape The Odds 

Escape the Odds Website Modernization 

1\. Project Overview 

Banyan Labs will design and develop a modern, conversion-focused marketing website for Escape the Odds that clearly communicates the organization's mission, impact, and offerings. The website will establish a professional online presence while supporting current operations and providing a scalable foundation for future growth. 

This project focuses on delivering immediate value through intentional simplicity, clean design, and clear calls-to-action that guide visitors toward meaningful engagement.  
2\. Scope of Work 

2.1 Website Design & Development 

Banyan Labs will deliver a custom-built marketing website featuring: 

● **Modern, Responsive Design** 

○ Custom design aligned with Escape the Odds brand identity 

○ Mobile-first approach ensuring optimal experience across all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile) 

○ Clean, professional aesthetic that builds credibility and trust 

● **Core Website Pages** 

○ Homepage with clear value proposition and primary calls-to-action ○ Mission & Impact page 

○ Courses overview page (with link to Go High Level curriculum platform) ○ Documentary overview page (marketing-focused) 

○ Blog section (powered by integrated CMS) 
- We are using PayloadCMS for the blog section

○ Contact/inquiry page 

○ Additional pages as defined in approved sitemap 

● **Navigation & Structure** 

○ Simplified, intuitive navigation 

○ Clear content hierarchy optimized for user journey 

○ Strategic placement of calls-to-action throughout site 

None 

`**Note:** The total scope for custom-designed and developed pages is limited to 10, one of which is the Blog landing page. This limit does not include individual blog posts created by the client through the integrated CMS.`  
2.2 Content Management System (CMS) 

● **Limited CMS Integration** 

○ Lightweight content management built in Next.js 

○ Blog section: Full ability to create, edit, and publish blog posts 

○ Select page sections: Ability to update text and images on designated pages without developer involvement 

○ Training documentation for CMS usage will be provided via the CMS website and some documentation from Banyan Labs to help get started. 

**Note:** CMS scope is intentionally limited to 2-3 sections of marketing content. Advanced content management features, complex workflows, or additional page editing capabilities would require Phase 2 scoping. 

2.3 Integrations 

● **Upcurve Donation Platform** (No Additional Cost) 

○ Setup of Escape the Odds Upcurve account 

○ Integration of Upcurve donation widget on website 

○ Configuration of donation campaigns and payment processing 

○ Basic training on Upcurve platform management 

● **Go High Level (GHL) Integration** 

○ Curriculum page will direct users to existing GHL platform 

○ Clean transition and user experience between website and GHL 

○ No deep embedding; link-based integration 

● **Google Analytics** 

○ Integration of Google Analytics tracking code (provided by client) 

○ Basic traffic and engagement tracking 

○ Foundation for future conversion tracking 

**Note:** Google Analytics scope is limited to basic traffic analytics. Advanced tagging, CTA tracking, funnel conversion tracking, and custom event tracking are out of scope for this phase. 

2.4 Technical Foundation 

● **Performance & Accessibility** 

○ Fast load times and optimized performance 

○ SEO-ready structure with proper meta tags and semantic HTML 

○ Clean, maintainable codebase 

● **Hosting Preparation** 

○ Website prepared for deployment on Hostinger (or client-selected hosting as long as the tech stack we are building the site in is supported) 

○ Deployment configuration and support 

○ Version-controlled codebase with automated deployment workflows

6\. Assumptions & Constraints 

This SOW is based on the following assumptions: 

1\. **Content Readiness** 

○ Client has existing copy and content ready or can provide within Week 1 ○ Content requires minimal editing or copywriting from Banyan Labs ○ Client will provide all necessary brand assets and images 

2\. **Hosting** 

○ Client will establish and maintain Hostinger hosting account (or approved alternative) 

○ Client will provide administrative access for deployment 

○ Hosting costs are outside this SOW and paid directly by client 

3\. **Third-Party Accounts** 

○ Client is responsible for creating and managing Google Analytics account ○ Client is responsible for providing GHL domain information 

○ Upcurve account will be created by Banyan Labs (no cost to client) 4\. **Scope Boundaries** 

○ Phase 1 scope is intentionally constrained to meet budget 

○ CMS functionality limited to blog and designated page sections 

○ Google Analytics integration is basic (traffic only, no advanced tagging) ○ Advanced features deferred to optional Phase 2 

5\. **Technical Approach** 

○ Website built using Next.js framework 

○ Limited CMS built in Next.js (not a full third-party CMS like WordPress) ○ Focus on marketing website functionality, not complex application features 6\. **Go High Level** 

○ Curriculum page will link to external GHL platform 

○ No deep GHL embedding or API integration 

○ Client manages all GHL content independently  
7\. Out of Scope 

The following items are **not included** in this engagement and would require separate scoping: 

● Custom course management or learning management system (LMS) ● Video DRM or protected content delivery systems 

● Deep Google Analytics configuration (custom events, conversion tracking, advanced tagging) 

● Deep Go High Level integration or API connections 

● Email marketing automation or CRM integration beyond Upcurve 

● Custom application development or database-driven features 

● Ongoing content creation, copywriting, or photography 

● Advanced CMS features or unlimited page editing capabilities 

● Search engine optimization (SEO) services or content strategy 

● Social media integration or management 

● E-commerce functionality beyond Upcurve donations 

● Video production or editing services 

8\. Acceptance Criteria 

The project will be considered complete and final payment due when: 

1\. All pages identified in approved sitemap are deployed and functional 2\. Website is responsive and functional across major browsers and devices 3\. CMS is operational for blog management and designated page sections 4\. Upcurve donation widget is integrated and functional 

5\. GHL curriculum page linking is implemented and tested 

6\. Google Analytics tracking code is integrated (using client-provided code) 7\. Website is deployed to production hosting environment 

8\. Client has completed training on CMS and Upcurve 

9\. Client has approved website for public launch
