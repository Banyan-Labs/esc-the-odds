# Escape the Odds: Vision and Scope Document

## Executive Summary

**Value Statement**: A streamlined, high-conversion web presence designed to centralize storytelling, education, and secure media distribution. We are transforming the Escape the Odds website into a **Dynamic Marketing Engine** that prioritizes revenue drivers (Courses, Documentary) while offloading operations to specialized tools (GoHighLevel, UpCurve).

---

## 1. Problem Statement

The current digital footprint reflects an earlier startup phase, leading to "content noise" that obscures current business drivers.

- **Diluted Messaging**: High-value revenue generators (Trucking Course, Documentary) compete with legacy content.
- **Administrative Friction**: Lack of seamless connection between public site and GoHighLevel (GHL).
- **Media Security Risks**: Need for DRM to protect the documentary for institutional licensing.
- **Information Overload**: Visitors (justice-impacted individuals, correctional facilities) struggle to find their specific CTA.

---

## 2. Desired Outcomes

- **Concise Digital Presence**: Modern layout focusing strictly on Mission, Courses, Documentary, and Donations.
- **Seamless GHL Integration**: Direct data flow to GoHighLevel for lead capture.
- **Secure Content Distribution**: Professional video hosting (Vimeo) with privacy controls.
- **Monetization**: Direct integration with **UpCurve** for donations.

---

## 3. Solution Overview

Banyan Labs will deploy a **Dynamic Marketing Engine** following a "User Experience (UX) First" methodology.

- **Front Door**: Custom-built marketing site (Next.js + Tailwind).
- **Engines**:
  - **GoHighLevel**: Lead processing and Course delivery.
  - **Vimeo**: Secure media hosting.
  - **UpCurve**: Donation management.

---

## 4. Target Customer Fit

**This solution is for:**

- Organizations needing a modern marketing website quickly.
- Brands ready to prioritize "money makers" over legacy content.
- Teams wanting automated workflows (GHL) rather than manual admin.

---

## 5. Core Capabilities (Phase-Based)

### Phase 1 – Rapid Foundation (The MVP)

**Timeline**: 2–4 Weeks | **Investment**: ~$2,500
**Primary Focus**: Clarity, Credibility, Conversion.

- **UX Audit & Content Mapping**: Removing legacy tabs (Podcast/Speaking).
- **Modern Homepage**: Prioritizing "Trucking Course" and "Film Screenings".
- **Basic Integrations**:
  - **Donations**: UpCurve (Stripe-connected).
  - **Lead Capture**: Basic GHL forms.
- **Responsive Design**: Mobile-first for all audiences.

### Phase 2 – Professional Integration & DRM

**Timeline**: 6–8 Weeks
**Primary Focus**: Security and Automation.

- **Advanced GHL Sync**: Full API documentation and course management.
- **Secure Media Vault**: Vimeo DRM for institutional licensing.
- **Product Onboarding**: Setup for Jonah and AI Grant-Writing tool.

### Phase 3 – Maintenance & Operations

**Focus**: Ongoing support, security monitoring, and feature updates.

---

## 6. Implementation Approach

We follow a phased milestone-driven delivery model.

**Phase 1 Milestones**:

1. **UX Audit**: Define layout, remove legacy content.
2. **Content Migration**: Move essential media from Wix.
3. **Basic Integrations**: Connect UpCurve and GHL forms.

---

## 7. Assumptions & Constraints

- Phase 1 is a "Rapid Foundation" MVP.
- Advanced DRM and custom LMS features are deferred to Phase 2.
- Content will be prioritized for the "Trucking Course" and "Documentary".
