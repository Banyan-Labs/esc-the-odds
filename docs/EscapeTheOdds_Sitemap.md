# Escape The Odds: Strategic Sitemap & Architecture

This sitemap is designed using the **Storybrand Framework** and the **Client Revamp** requirements. The focus is shifted from a "bio" site to a **Mission-Driven Brand** where the client (Institution or Individual) is the hero and ETO is the Guide.

## Core Navigation Structure

### 1. Home (The Gateway)

- **Purpose:** Instant clarity. Hook the visitor with the problem and the immediate solution.
- **Key Sections:**
  - Cinematic Hero (The Problem/Solution)
  - The "As Seen In" Authority Strip (Social Proof)
  - The Mission (Spotlighting Lived Experience)
  - The "Intensive" Teaser (Documentary shortcut)
  - Audience Pathing (Institutions | Corporate | Individual)
- **Primary CTA:** BOOK A SCREENING
- **Secondary CTA:** WATCH TRAILER

### 2. The Film & Screening (The Movement)

- **Purpose:** Showcase "The Film" and the "3-Hour Intensive" as the primary vehicle for change.
- **Key Sections:**
  - Trailer Lightbox
  - The Experience (Screening | Intermission | Q&A breakdown)
  - Screening Outcomes (Evidence-based impact)
  - Booking Flow (Direct link to calendar/form)
- **Primary CTA:** BOOK A SCREENING

### 3. Courses & Curriculum (The Toolkit)

- **Purpose:** Provide the structured tools (Digital & In-Person) for the transition.
- **Key Sections:**
  - The Bounce Forward Plan (Institutional/In-person curriculum)
  - Digital Academy (Box Truck Masterclass, Real Estate Hack)
  - Upcoming Workshops (Commercial Cleaning, etc.)
- **Primary CTA:** ACCESS COURSES

### 4. Partner With Us (The Collaboration)

- **Purpose:** Attract investors and institutional partners without "charity" language.
- **Key Sections:**
  - The Expansion Roadmap
  - Investor & Strategic Partner Opportunities
  - Partner Resource Portal (Coming Soon teaser)
- **Primary CTA:** COLLABORATE WITH US

### 5. Blog (The Dispatch)

- **Purpose:** Document the journey, share insights, and provide value to the community.
- **Key Sections:**
  - Featured Insights (Mindset, Business, Courses)
  - Scaling Updates (Roadmap progress)
  - Thought Leadership on Reentry
- **Primary CTA:** READ LATEST DISPATCH

### 6. About (The Mission & Authority)

- **Purpose:** Establish ETO as the "Guide" through lived experience and results.
- **Key Sections:**
  - The Story (Mission-driven narrative)
  - Testimonials & Impact Data
  - Media Features
- **Primary CTA:** JOIN THE MOVEMENT

### 7. Contact (Direct Support)

- **Purpose:** Simple, direct path to administration.
- **Primary CTA:** SUBMIT INQUIRY

---

## Technical Architecture Notes

- **Single Unified Call to Action:** "BOOK A SCREENING" should be present in the Header at all times.
- **SEO Focus:** High visibility for "Recidivism Reduction", "Reentry Curriculum", and "Evidence-based Reentry".
- **Mobile-First:** Optimized for quick browsing by facility administrators and busy professionals.
