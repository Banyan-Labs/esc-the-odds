# User Journeys & CTA Strategy

This document outlines the strategic paths users will take through the new Escape The Odds website. We have optimized these paths based on the **Storybrand Framework**, ensuring we address the user's problem and guide them to a clear solution.

## 1. The Institutional Journey (DOC, Facilities, Jails)

**The Profile:** Decision-makers at correctional facilities looking for effective, evidence-based recidivism reduction programs.

- **Entrance:** Landing on the Home page via a referral or search.
- **The Hook:** Cinematic Hero section displaying the "Master the Transition" headline.
- **The Path:**
  1.  User clicks **"BOOK A SCREENING"** (Primary CTA) or navigates to **"THE FILM"**.
  2.  Views the Trailer and reads about the "3-Hour Intensive" experience.
  3.  User checks the **"CURRICULUM"** page to verify the "Bounce Forward Plan" for internal implementation.
- **The Conversion:** Completes the Screening Booking form or schedules a discovery call.
- **CTA Strategy:** Use high-authority language. "Implement the Blueprint" vs "Buy the book".

## 2. The Strategic Partner Journey (Investors, Corporate)

**The Profile:** Investors or organizations looking to support workforce development and second-chance hiring.

- **Entrance:** Landing on the Home page or the "Partner With Us" page.
- **The Hook:** Seeing the "Partner Logos" (Social Proof) and the "Expansion Roadmap".
- **The Path:**
  1.  User navigates to **"PARTNER WITH US"**.
  2.  Reads about strategic collaboration (NOT donations).
  3.  Sees the breadth of courses (Box Truck, Real Estate) as evidence of the ecosystem's viability.
- **The Conversion:** Clicks **"COLLABORATE WITH US"** to initiate a high-level partnership inquiry.
- **CTA Strategy:** Focus on ROI—both social and economic. "Invest in Transition" vs "Give to ETO".

## 3. The Individual Journey (Students, Reentry Seekers)

**The Profile:** Individuals looking for immediate, actionable skills and a fresh start.

- **Entrance:** Often landing via social media or direct search for the "Box Truck Course".
- **The Hook:** The "Rewrite the Narrative" headline and the "As Seen In" media features.
- **The Path:**
  1.  User navigates to **"COURSES"**.
  2.  Browses specific offerings (Box Truck, Real Estate Hack).
  3.  Reads testimonials from other successful "Odds Escapers".
- **The Conversion:** Clicks **"ACCESS COURSES"** (leading to Amazon or GoHighLevel funnels).
- **CTA Strategy:** Empowering and urgent. "Take Control" or "Start Your Journey".

## Global CTA Hierarchy

### Primary: "BOOK A SCREENING"

- **Placement:** Global Header (Gold Button), Home Hero, Screening Page Page.
- **Goal:** Drive the core business objective (In-person/Virtual screenings).

### Secondary: "ACCESS COURSES"

- **Placement:** Home "Audience" section, Courses Page.
- **Goal:** Monetize the digital assets and provide immediate tools.

### Tertiary: "COLLABORATE WITH US"

- **Placement:** Footer, Partner Page.
- **Goal:** Build the long-term infrastructure and expansion.

## UX/UI Design Principles Applied

- **Dark Cinematic Aesthetic:** Creates a sense of weight, importance, and urgency.
- **Negative Experience -> Positive Outcome:** We start by acknowledging the "lifetime debt" (The Problem) and end with the "Escape" (The Solution).
- **Zero Friction Navigation:** The user should never be more than two clicks away from a conversion point.
- **Mobile Optimized:** Large touch targets for buttons and readable typography for mobile users.
